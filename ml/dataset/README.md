# Textile News Dataset Documentation

## Overview
This dataset is developed specifically for the **Textile Industry Fake News Detection System** capstone project. Because there is no standard, publicly available benchmark dataset exclusively covering textile industry misinformation, this dataset comprises curated industry news articles and structured synthetic examples designed for academic Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML) research.

## Purpose & Disclaimer
- **Academic Research Only**: This dataset is constructed to train, evaluate, and benchmark text classification algorithms (Logistic Regression, Multinomial Naive Bayes, Random Forest).
- **Not Ground Truth for Real-World Decisions**: Predictions generated by models trained on this dataset reflect statistical text patterns and stylistic signals, not mathematical proof of real-world veracity.
- **Dataset Labeling**:
  - `REAL`: Factual, authoritative-style news and press releases regarding market trends, trade agreements, production data, sustainability reports, and technology advancements.
  - `FAKE`: Fabricated, exaggerated, unverified rumors, speculative claims, sensationalist headlines, or fraudulent subsidy announcements related to the textile sector.
  - `data_type`: Explicitly flags whether an entry is `SYNTHETIC` (synthesized academic pattern) or `CURATED_ACADEMIC`.

## Columns Schema
| Column | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | Integer | Unique identifier for each news item |
| `title` | String | Headline of the news article |
| `text` | String | Full body text of the article / press release |
| `category` | String | Textile sector domain (Cotton, Silk, Wool, Yarn, Fabrics, Garments, Machinery, Subsidies, Policy, Prices, Sustainability, Labour, Technology) |
| `source` | String | News outlet or publication entity |
| `date` | String | Publication date (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `label` | String | Classification label (`REAL` or `FAKE`) |
| `data_type` | String | `SYNTHETIC` or `CURATED_ACADEMIC` |

## Covered Categories
1. **Cotton & Natural Fibers**: Minimum Support Price (MSP), harvest estimates, pest infestations, export bans/tariffs.
2. **Silk & Sericulture**: Mulberry production, cocoon auction rates, raw silk import customs.
3. **Wool & Specialty Fibers**: Merino wool imports, thermal apparel manufacturing, sheep breeding grants.
4. **Yarn & Spinning**: Spun yarn price volatility, spinning mill power subsidies, inventory stockpiling.
5. **Fabrics & Weaving/Knitting**: Powerloom modernization, grey fabric demand, synthetic blends.
6. **Garments & Apparel**: Export order volumes to EU/US, fast-fashion regulatory compliance, retailer earnings.
7. **Textile Machinery & Automation**: Air-jet loom upgrades, automated spinning machinery, robotics in cutting.
8. **Government Schemes & Subsidies**: PM MITRA parks, TUFS (Technology Upgradation Fund), RoDTEP rates, fictitious subsidy scams.
9. **Textile Policies & Trade**: Free Trade Agreements (FTA), antidumping duties, tariff changes.
10. **Prices & Commodities**: Cotton Corporation of India (CCI) auctions, synthetic fiber price swings.
11. **Supply Chain & Logistics**: Container shortages, freight rates, port congestion impacts on textile shipments.
12. **Sustainability & ESG**: Recycled polyester certification, organic cotton traceability, zero liquid discharge (ZLD).
13. **Labour & Industry Standards**: Minimum wage revisions, working hours compliance, strike disruptions.
14. **Textile Technology & Smart Textiles**: Conductive yarns, anti-microbial fabric finishes, nanotech textiles.
